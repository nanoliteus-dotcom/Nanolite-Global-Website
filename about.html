About Us

Nanolite Group of Companies. Established in 2010.

Our Heritage

Nanolite Group of Companies was founded in 2010 by the visionary entrepreneur Muhammad Momen, who recognized the need for innovative and sustainable solutions in the construction industry. Since our inception, we have consistently pushed the boundaries of technology and creativity, leading to our emergence as a pioneer in the lightweight construction materials sector.

Our Vision

At Nanolite Group, our vision is to be the leading provider of innovative, sustainable, and high-quality products across multiple industries. We aim to revolutionize construction practices with our CLC blocks while making significant strides in textiles, technology, mining, and agriculture. Our commitment to research and development ensures that we stay ahead of market trends and continuously improve our offerings.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver exceptional value to our customers through innovative products, unparalleled service, and sustainable practices. We strive to foster strong relationships with our stakeholders while creating a positive impact on the communities we serve. By embracing cutting-edge technology and sustainable practices, we aim to enhance the quality of life for our customers and contribute to a healthier planet.

Message from Our CEO & Founder, Muhammad Momen

"As the founder of Nanolite Group, I am proud to lead a company that embodies innovation, sustainability, and integrity. From our beginnings as a small startup specializing in Cellular Lightweight Concrete (CLC), we have grown into a diversified conglomerate that is committed to making a positive impact on the industries we serve. Our team’s dedication to research and development has enabled us to create superior products that not only meet the demands of our customers but also adhere to the highest standards of sustainability.

At Nanolite, we believe in the power of collaboration, continuous learning, and embracing new technologies. Our commitment to excellence drives us to explore new horizons, and I am excited about the future of our company and the opportunities that lie ahead."

Message from Our Chairman, Malik Mehmood

Commitment to Community

Nanolite Group is dedicated to giving back to the community. We actively engage in various corporate social responsibility initiatives aimed at promoting education, sustainability, and social welfare. Through partnerships with local organizations and educational institutions, we aim to make a lasting impact in the communities we serve.

Our Flagship Product: The Journey of CLC

At the heart of Nanolite’s success is our flagship product, Cellular Lightweight Concrete (CLC) Blocks, the culmination of over 9 years of dedicated research and development. With over 200 unique formulas, our CLC blocks represent an unparalleled innovation in lightweight construction materials.
