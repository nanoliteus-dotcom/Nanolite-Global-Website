Nanolite App Account and Data Deletion

Updated 11 October 2026

Draft for the planned Nanolite app release; not yet the published release notice.

Privacy policyTermsAccount & data deletion

Request deletion

To request deletion of your Nanolite app account or personal data, email nanoliteus@gmail.com with the subject “Nanolite app deletion request”. Include the email used for the app, your workspace name if known, and whether you request account deletion or deletion of specific information. You may request deletion without installing the app. Do not include passwords, verification codes or payment-card information.

Verification and handling

The operator will verify the request using proportionate information and explain any further verification needed. The actual production workflow and response deadlines must be implemented before this page becomes the public store deletion page. An email link alone is not represented as completing the Apple/Google in-app deletion requirement.

What may remain

Deletion should remove or anonymize account/profile information and unnecessary uploads. Required transaction, collection, financial and audit records may be retained only for documented legal or operational reasons, with applicable periods explained. Removing a team member merely disables access and is not account/data deletion. Production retention and backup expiry still require confirmation.
