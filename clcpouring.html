Made for your project.

Nanolite CLC Pouring is a specialized on-site concrete placement service designed for projects requiring lightweight, thermally efficient, and precisely controlled Cellular Lightweight Concrete (CLC). Using Nano ReadyMix®, Nanolite Ready Foaming Agent, and advanced mixing equipment, our experienced team produces fresh CLC directly on-site and pumps it into the required area with speed and accuracy.

Applications

  • Roof Insulation
  • Floor Screeds
  • Void Filling
  • Sunken Slabs
  • Lightweight Structural Concrete
  • Pipeline Backfilling
  • Bridge Approaches
  • Road Embankments
  • Thermal Insulation
  • Infrastructure Projects

Available Options

  • On-Site CLC Production
  • CLC Pumping & Placement
  • Roof Insulation Solutions
  • Lightweight Floor Filling
  • Custom Density Mixes
  • Project-Specific Pouring Services
  • Custom dimensions are available upon request

Site Requirements

Before pouring begins, the work area should be prepared and accessible for equipment and material delivery. Utilities, formwork, and project specifications should be finalized to ensure an efficient pouring process. Our team will coordinate with your site engineers to achieve the required density, volume, and placement quality.

Project Planning & Delivery

Nanolite provides complete project planning, scheduling, material supply, and on-site technical support for CLC pouring services. From small residential applications to large commercial and infrastructure developments, every project is planned to meet your construction schedule and engineering requirements.
