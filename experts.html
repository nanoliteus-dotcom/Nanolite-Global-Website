Frequently asked questions
Is technical consultation free?
Initial consultations and general technical inquiries are provided free of charge. More comprehensive engineering services or project-specific consulting may be quoted separately depending on the scope of work.
Can you review our project drawings?
Yes. Our engineers can review architectural and structural drawings and provide recommendations related to Nanolite products and construction systems.
Do you provide manufacturing plant consulting?
Yes. We assist clients with complete plant planning, machinery selection, production line design, commissioning, process optimization, and operator training.
Can international clients request consultations?
Absolutely. We work with clients worldwide and regularly conduct consultations through video meetings and online collaboration platforms.
How quickly will I receive a response?
General inquiries are typically answered within one business day. Technical consultations requiring engineering review are generally responded to within two to three business days.
Can you visit our project site?
Site visits may be arranged for qualified projects, subject to location, project scope, and scheduling availability.
