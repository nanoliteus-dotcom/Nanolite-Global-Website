Overview

Nanolite Ready Foaming Agent is a premium foaming solution specifically engineered for the production of high-quality Cellular Lightweight Concrete (CLC). Developed to produce stable, uniform, and long-lasting foam, it ensures consistent density, improved workability, and reliable performance throughout the manufacturing process.

Applications

  • Cellular Lightweight Concrete (CLC)
  • Roof Insulation
  • Lightweight Floor Screeds
  • Void Filling
  • Lightweight Structural Concrete
  • Thermal Insulation
  • Precast Lightweight Products

Packaging Options

  • 20 L Container
  • 50 L Container
  • 200 L Industrial Drum

Storage & Shelf Life

Nanolite Ready Foaming Agent in its original sealed container in a cool, dry, and well-ventilated area away from direct sunlight, excessive heat, and freezing conditions. Keep the container tightly closed when not in use to prevent contamination. When stored under recommended conditions, the product maintains its quality and performance throughout its specified shelf life. Always follow Nanolite's storage and handling recommendations to ensure optimum product performance.
