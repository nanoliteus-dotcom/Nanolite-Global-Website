T-500 Plant

The T-500 is designed for startups and small businesses looking to enter the lightweight concrete industry with a modest investment. It is compact, easy to operate, and ideal for producing blocks for local construction projects or internal company use.

T-1000 Plant

The T-1000 is built for businesses requiring greater production capacity while maintaining excellent product consistency. It offers improved workflow, higher efficiency, and increased daily output, making it suitable for growing manufacturers and commercial construction companies.

T-5000 Franchise
