Nanolite Establishment Consortium™

Established in 2010, Nanolite is a leading innovator in the construction industry, specializing in Cellular Lightweight Concrete (CLC) solutions. Our core product range includes CLC blocks, ready-made panels, and CLC ready-mix bags. We stand out for our ability to offer customized CLC blocks tailored to client specifications, including size, PSI, thermal insulation, and weight.

Lightweight Concrete (CLC)

During the last decades there is an increasing trend towards the use of lightweight materials in constructions and infrastructures. In that sense concrete is a material with an intermediate density of around 1500 kg/m³.

Learn more

Precast Panels (PCP)

Precast concrete insulated wall panels are designed for use in many interior/exterior and load bearing or non-load bearing building applications and provide a professional finished look as well as increased thermal properties.

Learn more

Nano Hybrid Walls

Nano Hybrid Wall is an innovative wall construction system made to address one of the biggest challenges in the Pakistani construction industry. Nanolite CLC is poured into the wall cavity. Once cured, the shuttering is removed, leaving a smooth surface that requires little to no plaster.

Learn more

Insulated Roof Tiles

Nanolite Insulated Roof Tiles are advanced lightweight roofing solutions engineered to provide superior thermal insulation, durability, and energy efficiency, lowering indoor temperatures, decreasing cooling costs and placing significantly less load on the building structure.

Learn more

Nanolite Porous Concrete

Nanolite Porous Concrete is a specialized permeable concrete designed for roads, pathways, parking areas, and pedestrian walkways, allowing rainwater to pass through the surface and naturally recharge the ground below.

Learn more

Nanolite Ready Mix Concrete

Nanolite Ready Mix Concrete Bags are specially formulated dry-mix cementitious products engineered to simplify the production of high-performance cellular lightweight concrete (CLC). Manufactured under strict quality control standards, each bag contains a precise blend of cement, additives, and performance-enhancing materials designed to deliver consistent strength, workability, and lightweight properties.
