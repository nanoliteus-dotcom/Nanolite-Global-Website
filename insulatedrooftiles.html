Overview

Nanolite Insulated Roof Tiles are advanced lightweight roofing solutions designed to improve thermal efficiency while reducing the structural load on buildings. Manufactured using Nanolite Cellular Lightweight Concrete (CLC) technology, these roof tiles help maintain comfortable indoor temperatures, reduce heat transfer, and contribute to lower energy consumption throughout the year.

Storage & Handling

Roof tiles should be stored on a clean, level surface and protected from impact during transportation and handling. Stacking should follow recommended guidelines to prevent edge damage before installation. Proper storage helps preserve product quality and ensures safe installation on-site.

Supply & Delivery

Nanolite Insulated Roof Tiles are available for individual projects, bulk commercial orders, and export shipments. Products are securely packaged and prepared for safe transportation, with scheduled deliveries available to meet project timelines and construction requirements.
