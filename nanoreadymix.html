Overview

Designed for consistent performance and professional-grade CLC production, Nano ReadyMix® is Nanolite's proprietary dry mix engineered for manufacturers, contractors, and construction professionals. Every bag is formulated to simplify production while delivering reliable density, improved workability, and outstanding strength.

Available packaging

40 kg bags. Pakistan material price: PKR 600 per bag, equivalent to PKR 15/kg.

Applications

  • Roof Insulation

Compatible With

  • Nanolite Manufacturing Plants
  • Most Standard CLC Production Systems

Compliance & Quality

Nano ReadyMix® is manufactured under strict internal quality control procedures to ensure consistent performance and reliable production. Every batch is carefully inspected before packaging to maintain high manufacturing standards.

Storage & Shelf Life

Store bags in a cool, dry environment away from moisture and direct sunlight. Keep packaging sealed until use and store on pallets for maximum protection. When stored correctly, Nano ReadyMix® maintains its quality throughout its recommended shelf life.
