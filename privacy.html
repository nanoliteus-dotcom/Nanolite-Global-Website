Privacy / Nanolite Global

Website privacy policy.

Information about this website and how inquiries are handled.

Scope and contact

This policy covers the Nanolite Global website and its published preview. For privacy questions, contact management@nanolite.co. It does not replace the separate privacy policy for the Nanolite app.

Information you choose to provide

Inquiry and order forms ask for contact details and information about your question, product requirements or project. Please provide only information needed to discuss your request. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive documents.

How website forms work

Preparing an inquiry or order review processes the fields in your browser. The current website does not upload these form fields to a form database. Choosing Open email draft passes the reviewed information to your selected email application. Your message reaches Nanolite when you send that email.

Hosting and technical information

Cloudflare hosts and protects this website and may process request information, such as IP addresses and browser or connection details, to deliver and secure it. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for information about its services.

Cloudflare privacy policy

Cookies and local storage

The website does not use advertising trackers or store form entries. The currency selector may cache public exchange rates in session storage. Currency conversion contacts ExchangeRate-API, which receives normal connection information. Your browser may also cache pages, fonts and media.

Use of correspondence

When you send an email, the information in it is used to handle your inquiry, discuss products or services, prepare quotations and maintain relevant business correspondence. Email providers process messages as part of delivery. Information may also be handled where necessary to meet applicable obligations or address security and disputes.

Your choices

You can choose not to complete a form, edit your draft before sending it, or contact us directly. To ask about personal information you have sent to Nanolite, request a correction, or ask for deletion, email management@nanolite.co. Handling may require proportionate identity verification and consideration of relevant record-keeping obligations.

Payments

Order Now links for ReadyMix and foaming agent open Square checkout. Square collects and processes payment information on its own checkout page; the Nanolite website does not receive card details. Converted reference prices do not change the fixed Square checkout amount.

Links and the app

Links to research publications, other websites and app platforms take you to independently operated services. Their privacy practices apply there. Nanolite app notices describe app-specific processing separately.

Updates

This notice reflects the current website. We will update it if website features or data handling change. Last updated: 12 October 2026.
