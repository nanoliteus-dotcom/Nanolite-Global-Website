ASTM C595/C595M

Blended hydraulic cements

Blended hydraulic cements combine Portland cement or clinker with constituents such as slag, pozzolan or limestone. The specification addresses composition and performance requirements for general and special applications.

ASTM C90

Loadbearing concrete masonry units

This specification covers machine-made, dry-cast concrete masonry in lightweight, medium-weight and normal-weight classes. Its requirements are specific to the materials and manufacturing process within its scope.
