T-1000 Plant

A portable plant for growing manufacturers

Overview

The T-1000 Plant uses manual production for cellular lightweight concrete, with a nominal output of 1,000 blocks per day. The configuration combines slurry and foam mixing with battery moulds, without a cutting machine or bulk silos.

Production capacity

Nominal output: 1,000 blocks per day. Actual output depends on block dimensions, mix design, shift length, mould availability, curing and handling. Confirm the operating specification with our team.

Mixing & foam control

The hollow-bore shaft spiral mixer is designed to distribute pre-generated foam through the slurry with controlled mixing, helping preserve the cellular structure. Mix design, dosing, mixing time and quality checks are established during commissioning.

Operation

Manual production configuration. Site layout, utilities and staffing are planned around the agreed equipment specification.

Equipment configuration

  • Slurry mixer
  • Foam generation and mixing system
  • Battery moulds
