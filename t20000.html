Overview

The T-20000 Franchise is a cellular lightweight concrete production system with a nominal output of 20,000 blocks per day. The configuration includes bulk silos, slurry mixing and foam generation for coordinated material preparation.

Production capacity

Nominal output: 20,000 blocks per day. Actual output depends on block dimensions, mix design, shift length, mould availability, curing and handling. Confirm the operating specification with our team.

Mixing & foam control

The hollow-bore shaft spiral mixer is designed to distribute pre-generated foam through the slurry with controlled mixing, helping preserve the cellular structure. Mix design, dosing, mixing time and quality checks are established during commissioning.

Equipment configuration

  • Slurry mixer
  • Foam generation and mixing system
  • Bulk storage silos
  • Moulding and handling requirements selected for your site
